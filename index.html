      Content · Block-bottom paper bag

      Reference card for the trainer. Steps and timings follow the reference video; dimensions match the 2D model (one grid square on the cutting mat is 1 cm).

      Procedure

        Sheet specification

        Paper
        Kraft, 80–100 GSM
        Flat sheet
        25.4 × 20.0 cm
        Glue flap
        1.4 cm
        Flat tube width
        12.0 cm
        Bottom allowance
        4.5 cm
        Finished bag
        8 W × 4 D × 14 H cm
        Handles
        2 cords, knotted ends

        Tools

        Liquid glue, single-hole punch, cotton or paper cord, self-healing cutting mat with 1 cm grid.

        Quality checks

        Seam glued along the full length, bottom diamond flaps meet in the centre, sharp gusset creases, holes level on both faces, knots large enough not to pull through.
